Outreach & policy

I engage with policy makers and the public on Arctic climate and air quality:

Policy

Co-authored policy briefs on polar climate and aerosol–cloud interactions through CRiceS and CERTAINTY, aimed at climate resilience planning.

Book

Chapter “Sea ice, aerosols and clouds” for a broad scientific audience in Sea Ice: Its Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geology and Societal Importance.

Public

The CERTAINTY project shares its science publicly — including an aerosol–cloud quiz, videos and open datasets for schools and citizens.
