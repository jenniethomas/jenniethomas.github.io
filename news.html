News

Recent papers, projects and events. Follow along on X / Twitter.

2026

Perspective paper “Aerosol–cloud interactions: Overcoming a barrier to projecting near-term climate evolution and risk” published in AGU Advances, as part of CERTAINTY.

2026

The EU H2020 project CRiceS concluded after four years — publications, datasets and policy briefs remain openly available via the project's Zenodo community.

2026

New papers on Arctic aerosols and clouds: “Marine secondary aerosols are required for modeling clouds in the Arctic” (GRL) and “The biogenic sulfur cycle in the coupled ocean–sea ice–atmosphere system” (Elementa).

2025

Two papers in the Faraday Discussions on chemistry in the cold: “Ten crucial unknowns in atmospheric chemistry in the cold” and “The interplay between snow and polluted air masses in cold urban environments”.

2025

Book chapter “Sea ice, aerosols and clouds” published in Sea Ice: Its Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geology and Societal Importance.

2024

“Polar aerosol atmospheric rivers” (JGR Atmospheres) introduced the concept of polar aerosol atmospheric rivers — long-range transport events that can carry substantial aerosol loadings to the Arctic.

2023

CERTAINTY begins — I coordinate this Horizon Europe project on aerosol–cloud interactions with 18 partners across 10 countries.
