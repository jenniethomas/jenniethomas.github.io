OPERA research group @ IGE

Open computational earth and climate science research

OPERA uses open science methodologies to foster interdisciplinary research advancing understanding of key environmental and climate processes. The group works across three research themes — atmosphere (aerosols, clouds, atmosphere–surface interactions), ocean (near-surface processes, air–sea exchanges, remote sensing) and climate (modeling and prediction) — while advancing computational methods: process-based model development, calibration and uncertainty quantification, hybrid and data-driven modeling, and modular, interoperable model design.

OPERA is co-led by Jennie Thomas and Julien Le Sommer, and brings together senior researchers, postdoctoral researchers, PhD students and research engineers.

Supervision

Within OPERA, I supervise and co-supervise PhD students and postdoctoral researchers working on polar atmospheric chemistry, aerosol–cloud interactions and open modeling tools. The current roster is on the OPERA people page.
